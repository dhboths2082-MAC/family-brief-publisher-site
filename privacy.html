Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25. family-brief-publisher is a private tool used only by its developer (one person). It has no other users, no website accounts and no servers.

Google user data the tool accesses

  • Google Docs: the text and links of ten specific documents owned by the developer, in order to compare and update them.
  • Google Sheets: the rows of two tabs of one Sheet owned by the developer (read-only).
  • Google Drive: only the sharing list (who has access, and with which role) of those eleven files. File content is not read through Drive.
  • Gmail: the tool sends one email per week and reads back only the message it sent, plus a search of the Sent folder to avoid duplicates. It does not read or index any other email.

How the data is used and stored

  • All processing happens on the developer's own computer. Data is not sent to any server operated by the developer or by a third party other than Google itself.
  • The sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain of that computer. It is never written to logs or shared.
  • Data is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or transferred to anyone. No humans read it other than the developer.
  • The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local copies of document and email readbacks are kept on the developer's computer for the developer's own audit. The developer can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local files and the Keychain item.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone but the developer's own Google account.
